DronePrep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

DronePrep ("the app") is published by Talnir Apps (talnirapps@gmail.com).

What we collect

Nothing on our own servers. DronePrep has no accounts and no backend. Your study data — question progress, scores, streaks, mock-exam history, settings — is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process device information (such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data) to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's privacy policy.

  • Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent form is shown before any ad request, and you may choose non-personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On iOS, personalised ads additionally require your permission via App Tracking Transparency. Declining simply keeps ads non-personalised.
  • Ad content is restricted to Google's "G" (general audiences) rating.

Notifications

The optional daily study reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It involves no server and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for the Canadian drone pilot certificate exams (minimum age 14 for Basic Operations). It has no data collection of its own, and ad content is restricted to general audiences.

Not affiliated with Transport Canada

DronePrep is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Transport Canada or the Government of Canada, and it does not issue certificates. Always verify current regulations with official government sources.
