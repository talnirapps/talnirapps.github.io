LingoPath — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

LingoPath ("the app") is published by Talnir Apps (talnirapps@gmail.com).

What we collect

Nothing on our own servers. LingoPath has no accounts and no backend. Your learning data — course progress, streaks, XP, hearts, settings — is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process device information (such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data) to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's privacy policy.

  • Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent form is shown before any ad request, and you may choose non-personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • On iOS, personalised ads additionally require your permission via App Tracking Transparency. Declining simply keeps ads non-personalised.
  • Ad content is restricted to Google's "G" (general audiences) rating.

Notifications

The optional daily practice reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It involves no server and can be turned off in Settings.

Speech

Listening exercises use your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. No audio or text is sent to us.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children; it has no data collection of its own, and ad content is restricted to general audiences.
